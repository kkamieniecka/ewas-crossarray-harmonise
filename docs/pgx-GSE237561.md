# CYP pharmacogenomic variant calling on GSE237561 (stage 07)

Run: `bin/07_pgx_genotypes.R`, inputs `results/harmonised` (stage 01) and
`data/idat`; outputs `results/pgx/`, log
`results/GSE237561/logs/stage07_pgx.log`. 126 samples, 38 subjects, 92 on 450K
and 34 on EPIC.

## What a methylation array can and cannot genotype

Infinium 450K and EPIC are not genotyping arrays. No probe on either array
interrogates a CYP star allele: not CYP2D6 *2/*4/*10, not CYP2C19 *2/*17, not
CYP1A2 *1F. CYP2D6 structural variation — whole-gene deletion, duplication,
CYP2D6-CYP2D7 hybrids — is not assessed here either. A star-allele diplotype
and a metaboliser phenotype require targeted pharmacogene genotyping, an
imputed GWAS panel or sequencing, followed by a caller such as PharmCAT, Aldy
or StellarPGx. Nothing in this stage substitutes for that, and no output of it
should be read as a metaboliser status.

Two things the arrays do carry are called here.

**1. The rs identity probes.** 65 on 450K, 59 on EPIC, 59 in common; designed
to be genotyped rather than methylation-read, and distributed genome-wide
rather than over pharmacogenes. Their use is to confirm that the repeat samples
of a subject are the same person — the assumption every within-subject effect
in this pipeline rests on.

**2. Probes whose beta is driven by a SNP.** A variant under the probe body,
the single-base extension site or the interrogated CpG makes a probe report
genotype rather than methylation, producing a gapped, near-discrete beta
distribution. `minfi::gaphunter` is the standard detector; the 450K
annotation's `Probe_rs` / `SBE_rs` / `CpG_rs` columns say which hits have a
documented variant to explain them. Applied to the CYP probes, this is the set
of CYP-locus variants this experiment can genotype.

## Sample identity

Genotypes were called from the rs-probe allelic ratios at beta < 0.25 / > 0.75
(7,434 calls: 2,124 AA, 3,131 AB, 2,179 BB). Concordance over the 59 shared
probes separates cleanly:

| pairs | n | median concordance | extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| same subject | 158 | 1.000 | min 0.949 |
| different subjects | 7,717 | 0.373 | max 0.610 |

No sample swap or mislabelled repeat: the two distributions do not touch.

This check also exposes a design fact that matters for the harmonisation
claims. No subject appears on both arrays — all 92 450K samples and all 34 EPIC
samples come from disjoint sets of people, and the count of within-subject
cross-array pairs is zero. The array boundary is therefore never crossed
*within* a subject anywhere in this cohort: within-subject longitudinal
contrasts are array-pure by construction, and array is confounded with subject
at the between-subject level. That is the reason the pipeline's cross-array
agreement is measured by re-estimating effects per array rather than by
comparing repeat measurements of the same donor on the two platforms.

## CYP loci

668 probes in the harmonised matrix are annotated to a CYP gene, including
CYP1A2 (8 probes), CYP2D6 (10), CYP2C19 (5), CYP2C9 (4), CYP3A4 (6), CYP3A5
(2) and CYP2B6 (12). Five of the 668 show a genotype-like gap
(`--gap-threshold 0.15`, outlier cutoff 0.01):

| probe | gene | position (hg19) | clusters | documented SNP | sample split |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cg08561071 | CYP2F1 | chr19:41,627,236 | 2 | rs113512828, MAF 0.016 | 100 low / 26 mid |
| cg07512206 | CYP4F8 | chr19:15,725,989 | 2 | none | 61 mid / 65 high |
| cg14711976 | CYP4F12 | chr19:15,783,239 | 2 | none | 117 mid / 9 high |
| cg09199330 | CYP27C1 | chr2:127,963,503 | 2 | none | 3 mid / 123 high |
| cg10985347 | CYP27C1 | chr2:127,963,512 | 2 | none | 3 mid / 123 high |

None of these is a usable genotype. Every hit has two clusters, not the three a
biallelic SNP produces in a cohort this size; four have no annotated variant at
the probe, CpG or extension site; and the one that does, cg08561071, splits
100/26, far more carriers than an allele of frequency 0.016 can account for, so
the documented variant does not explain its bimodality either. Two-cluster
gaps of this kind are the expected behaviour of probes with a small number of
extreme samples or genuinely bimodal methylation, which is why `gaphunter`
reports groups rather than genotypes.

The clinically actionable pharmacogenes carry no gapped probe at all. The
conclusion for this cohort is flat: GSE237561 contains sample-identity genotype
information and no CYP pharmacogenetic information. Pharmacogenomic hypotheses
about clozapine metabolism in these subjects — CYP1A2 induction by smoking,
CYP2D6 poor-metaboliser status — cannot be tested on these data, and would need
genotypes deposited or generated separately.

What the arrays *can* support at these loci is methylation, not genotype: the
668 CYP probes are ordinary members of the harmonised matrix and can be taken
through the stage 02 within-subject model against `days_on_clozapine` as a
targeted pharmacoepigenetic analysis. That is a different question from variant
calling and is not run here.

## Reproducing

```
Rscript bin/07_pgx_genotypes.R \
  --in-dir results/harmonised \
  --sheet data/sample_sheets/sample_sheet_GSE237561.csv \
  --idat-dir data/idat --out-dir results/pgx
```

Outputs: `pgx_rs_genotypes.csv` (59 x 126 calls), `pgx_identity_concordance.csv`
(all 7,875 pairs), `pgx_cyp_variant_probes.csv`, `pgx_cyp_genotypes.csv`,
`pgx_cyp_beta.csv`, `pgx_summary.json`. Figure:
`docs/figures/fig_pgx_cyp_GSE237561.png`.
